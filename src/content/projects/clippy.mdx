## Oh My God, They Killed Clippy!

For most of my tenure at Microsoft, I was on the team that owned the help system in Microsoft Office, and that included Clippy (the assistant).

I didn't have anything to do with the creation of Clippy, but I was involved in removing it (him?) from Office.

Clippy arrived in Office-97, as an offshoot of Microsoft Bob!

In Office-XP (circa 2001), Clippy remained part of Office, but was off by default. Users had to enable it.

Finally, in Office-2007, my team removed Clippy altogether. I was the dev-manager of the team at the time, and always looking for opportunities to play in the code rather than do whatever it was that managers were supposed to do, so I did a very exhaustive search and destroy, yanking out any bits of source code related to the assistant.

The Office C codebase used [Hungarian Notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_notation), and the prefix for all things Clippy was `TFC`.

This, famously, was because Bill Gates referred to this feature as "That Fuzzy Clown", but "Fuzzy" was a different "F" word.

`TFC` provided a great trail to follow for my search and destroy mission. If someone wanted to bring Clippy back, I wanted to make sure they had to start from scratch. Really, I just enjoyed (and still enjoy) removing dead code. Most of what I did in 19 years at Microsoft was delete code, including most of the code I wrote. Hmmm...
