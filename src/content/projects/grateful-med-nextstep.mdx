Grateful Med was a DOS application produced by the NLM (National Library of Medicine) in the United States, enabling searching of the MEDLINE database.

At some point we were given access to the source code (Pascal and assembly language, if I recall correctly), and I created a NeXTSTEP port.

As far as I know, this was a sanctioned port, but it was never officially released and only used briefly by a handful of doctors and researchers at the University of Alberta Hospital.
