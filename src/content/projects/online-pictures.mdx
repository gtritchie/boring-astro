## Sequel to the Sequel

In Office-2013, we removed Clip Organizer entirely, and stopped shipping clipart assets with the product, instead leveraging online sources.

I believe I did the implementation of the new "Insert Pictures" dialog, which was really just a container for a web browser (Trident at the time) control. I had little to do with what was shown inside the rectangle; our web-devs who built and operated Office Online, and later Bing Images, did all of that.
